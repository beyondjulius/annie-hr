Offer

What gets installed.

The stack is ordered for scarcity — people systems and governance first. Virtual assistance is the delivery mode, not the product.

  1. 01

    People Ops Systems

    Build and run the employee lifecycle so leadership has one controlled source of workforce truth.

    • Employee records, onboarding, leave, and benefits coordination
    • HRIS structure and cleanup (including BambooHR centralisation experience)
    • Lifecycle administration across recruitment through ongoing employment records
  2. 02

    Talent Pipeline Ops

    Keep hiring moving without losing documentation, scheduling, or onboarding continuity.

    • Recruitment planning and candidate documentation
    • Interview and onboarding coordination with HR and business stakeholders
    • Pipeline trackers that show status through to completion
  3. 03

    Workforce Data & Reporting

    Turn attendance, leave, payroll inputs, and people data into management packs leadership can use.

    • Excel-based trackers and workforce reporting
    • Data quality checks, verification, and structured records
    • Statutory inputs covering PAYE, SSNIT, and pension data where required
  4. 04

    Governance Documentation

    Controlled documentation for regulated and audit-sensitive environments — not loose folder chaos.

    • Bank of Ghana PFTSP licensing documentation support
    • ISO/IEC 27001 ISMS records and audit evidence coordination
    • Policy, confidential information, and controlled-records discipline
  5. 05

    Executive & Ops Rhythm

    Keep decisions, actions, and stakeholder work visible through completion.

    • Meeting preparation, minutes, and action trackers
    • Management reports and presentations from operational information
    • Notion-based priority and deliverable visibility across concurrent workstreams
  6. 06

    Support Desk Discipline

    Secondary proof of operating quality — remote enquiry handling with speed, case records, and follow-through.

    • Structured troubleshooting and cross-functional follow-up
    • Digital case records for continuity
    • Documented response and satisfaction performance in live remote environments

Commercial packages and retainers are confirmed in conversation — this page defines the work, not pricing.
