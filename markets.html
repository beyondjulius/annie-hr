Markets

One standard. Three contexts.

Annie is Accra-rooted. The work travels. The buying context changes — the operating standard does not.

Ghana

For fintechs, regulated firms, and growing teams

When payroll inputs, employee records, HRIS structure, and regulator-facing documentation must hold under scrutiny, Annie brings local labour-system fluency plus Bank of Ghana and ISO documentation experience. Ideal when you need people ops that survive audit — not informal admin.

Africa

For remote and multi-country operators

When you need structured people ops without standing up a full HR department, Annie installs the trackers, lifecycle rhythm, and documentation discipline that keep distributed teams hireable and visible. Already delivered across Ghana–Nigeria remote patterns.

International

For founders and ops leads outside Africa

When you want Accra-timezone capacity that already works to remote US delivery standards, Annie has operated customer support and data workflows for a US platform while holding regulated Ghanaian HR responsibilities. English-first. Confidential. Systems-minded.

Not for

Where Annie is the wrong hire.

Pure receptionist or inbox-only gigs as the primary ask. Cheapest hourly VA with no systems ownership. Environments that treat confidential or regulated people data casually.
