About

Annie Opokua Yeboah

Human Capital · Operations · Data & Governance. Based in Tseaddo, Accra — delivering across Ghana, Africa, and international remote teams.

Annie is a Human Capital and Operations professional with 5+ years across fintech, technology, agribusiness, public-sector, and NGO environments. She combines employee lifecycle administration, workforce data, recruitment coordination, compliance documentation, and remote operating discipline.

The through-line is infrastructure: centralising records into systems leadership can trust, keeping hiring and onboarding moving, and maintaining controlled documentation in regulated settings — including Bank of Ghana PFTSP licensing support and ISO/IEC 27001 ISMS records.

She is currently pursuing an MSc in Human Resources Management at the University of Gold Coast (Accra Business School), building on a BA English from KNUST and a stack of practice credentials in people analytics, information security, and generalist HR.
